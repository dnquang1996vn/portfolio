## Samples

Quinn approved this voice on 2026-11-28 for the post `making-sure-a-feature-works`, after rejecting a
first draft as "giọng văn tiếng Việt không tự nhiên". The reference Quinn pointed to is the blog
Tôi đi code dạo (toidicodedao.com): take its manner, never its sentences.

What the voice does:

- Tells it to the reader: "mình" for Quinn, "các bạn" for readers, opens with "Chuyện là thế này".
- Longer sentences joined by commas, the way people talk, with a short one now and then.
- Spoken particles: "đấy", "đâu nhé", "thôi", "luôn", "có điều", "thế là", "nói đi cũng phải nói lại".
- A dry aside in parentheses, at most once or twice a post.
- Digits for numbers: "1 feature", "2 thứ", "3 agent".
- Ends by admitting the post is not complete and asking the readers.
